# Android Hands-Free Assistant — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 25 October 2026**

This policy describes how the Android hands-free voice assistant (package ID `com.mattyai.assistant`) handles information.

## Information processed on your device

- **Microphone audio and speech:** The app uses the microphone only after you enable hands-free listening or start a voice command. Speech recognition uses the bundled Vosk English (US) model on your device. The app does not send microphone recordings or recognition transcripts to the app developer's server.
- **Screen and accessibility information:** If you separately enable the Android Accessibility service, the app reads visible interface labels only to perform the explicit tap, long-press, typing, scrolling, swiping, Back, or Home action you requested. It does not upload screen contents to the developer. Android may display its own permission warnings.
- **Settings and consent:** Preferences such as hands-free enablement and acknowledgement of the accessibility disclosure are stored in the app's private local storage.
- **Bluetooth:** If you use the nearby-device screen, Android Bluetooth permissions are used to discover nearby Bluetooth devices. The app does not operate a developer-hosted Bluetooth data service.

## Actions that may use other services

If you ask the app to search the web or open an app-store search, it opens a browser or app store using the query you provided. The destination service then handles its own network request under its own privacy policy. The app does not proxy those searches through a developer server.

## Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The app does not include an app-developer account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated backend in this build. It does not sell personal information. Information you intentionally type or send in another app is handled by that other app.

## Your controls

You can turn off hands-free listening in the app, revoke microphone permission in Android settings, and disable the Accessibility service at any time in Android Accessibility settings. You can uninstall the app to remove its private local app data.

## Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect children's personal information. It does not knowingly collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

## Security and policy changes

Audio recognition is performed locally, but the app can interact with other apps when you explicitly request actions. Review commands before using them for messages, posts, payments, deletion, or other consequential actions. This policy should be updated if future versions add networked processing, analytics, accounts, or other data handling.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact details provided on the app's official store listing.
